Why Support?

SafeKeepVault is a community-driven project with no corporate backing, no venture capital, and no subscription plans. Development, testing, and maintenance are funded exclusively by the users who value the software.

Every contribution goes straight toward building new features, fixing bugs, and keeping the system stable. Our goal is simple: the software will always be free, open-source, and available to everyone, no matter what.

Non-Financial Contributions

SafeKeepVault is maintained by a dedicated developer. Your time and expertise are as valuable as your monetary contributions. You can directly impact the project’s evolution in the following ways:

  • Report a Bug: Found something broken? Let us know on GitHub. Telling us what computer you’re using and how the error happened helps us fix it faster.
  • Help Solve Issues: Take a look at our Open Issues. If you’ve run into the same problem as someone else, adding your experience helps track down the cause.
  • Check Our Work: If you’re comfortable with code, look over our Pull Requests. Testing new changes and giving feedback ensures the software stays solid for everyone.
  • Spread the Word: Tell other Bitcoiners about the project. In a world of loud ads, a simple recommendation from a friend is the best support we can get.
