## Title

SafeKeepVault in one minute: keep your bitcoin keys offline on a computer you already own

## Description

SafeKeepVault turns an ordinary laptop and a USB flash drive into an offline place to create, back up and use your bitcoin keys. It never connects to the internet, so hackers and malware can't reach your keys, and there's no hardware wallet to buy.

How it works:
1. Put SafeKeepVault on a USB flash drive (8 GB or more).
2. Start your computer from it. No internet, and your normal drive is left untouched.
3. Approve payments offline. Your wallet app (Sparrow, BlueWallet, Nunchuk, Electrum, Specter, Bitcoin Core) stays online; only QR codes or a USB drive cross the gap, never your keys.

Free and open source. Every release is signed.

Website and download: https://safekeepbitcoin.com
Online demo (preview only, demo seeds): https://demo.safekeepbitcoin.com
Source code: https://github.com/Pasalon0404/SafeKeepVault
