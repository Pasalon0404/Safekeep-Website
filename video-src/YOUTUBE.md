## Title

SafeKeepVault in a minute: keep your bitcoin keys offline on a computer you already own

## Description

SafeKeepVault turns an ordinary laptop and a USB flash drive into an offline place to create, back up and use your bitcoin keys. It never connects to the internet, so hackers and malware can't reach your keys, and there's no hardware wallet to buy.

How it works:
1. Put SafeKeepVault on a USB flash drive (8 GB or more).
2. Start your computer from it. All networking and disk access are removed when it boots.
3. Create seed words, passphrases and watch-only wallets, and review and sign bitcoin transactions, all offline on a full-size screen and keyboard.

Your wallet app (Sparrow, BlueWallet, Nunchuk, Electrum, Specter, Bitcoin Core) stays online. Your keys never do: only QR codes or a USB drive cross the gap, and payments come back signed.

Free and open source. Every release is signed.
